Quick note

Someone who grew up near Conway emailed this week after the timeline post, mostly to say the 1975 entry undersold how small that world was — that "everyone's cousin knew somebody who'd given him a ride at some point." I can't verify that as a documented fact, so I'm not adding it to the record, but it's the kind of texture I started this blog to collect, and I wanted to say so in public instead of just replying privately.

If you've got a story like that — something that isn't quite citable but that shaped how you think about the case — I want it. Doesn't need to be dramatic. Contact's on the About page. I read everything.

That's the note. More next week.
