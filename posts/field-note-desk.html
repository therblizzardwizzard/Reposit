What's still on my desk

A short one this week — no thesis, just what I'm stuck on.

By Marlowe Hatcher

Quick note

I'm not writing a full post this week. I'm still in the Wilton Earle book — third pass now — and I keep stalling on the same thing: how much of what Gaskins told Earle reads like a man performing for the tape, and how much of it Earle himself seems to have pushed back on in the margins. It's not a clean split. Some pages it's obvious; some pages I genuinely can't tell.

That's the whole note. I don't have a conclusion yet, and I'd rather say "I don't have a conclusion yet" than force one. If you've read it and have a take on where the performance starts, I'd actually like to hear it — email's here.

Back to a real post next week. Probably about Prospect itself, which I've been putting off because I want to get the geography right instead of vague.
